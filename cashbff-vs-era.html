neither bet is wrong. they're for different people. here's the honest side-by-side.

when era is the better pick.

if you already run your life through ai assistants, you're comfortable giving an ai write-access to your accounts, and "my money optimizes itself while i sleep" sounds like the dream, era is built precisely for you, and it's a serious product. power users with lots of accounts will feel at home.

when cashbff is the better pick.

if handing your money to an autopilot sounds less like a dream and more like one more thing happening to you, that instinct is worth trusting. for a lot of people, especially anyone for whom checking the balance already feels like bad news, the fix isn't less contact with your money. it's calmer contact.

cashbff is that. it does the looking you've been dreading and answers in plain language when you ask, in the same whatsapp you text your friends. nothing acts on your money in the background. nothing pings you. you build back trust with your own numbers, one calm look at a time, and every decision stays in your hands.
